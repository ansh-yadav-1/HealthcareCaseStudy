Executive Synthesis & Management Takeaways

1. Operational Equilibrium

The facility maintains a uniform patient profile (mean age 51.5 yrs) without demographic skew. The 6 major conditions distribute evenly (~9,100–9,200 each), and payer share is balanced (~20% per insurer). Multi-specialty resource allocation should remain balanced.

2. Financial Forecasting

With average encounter billing holding steady around $25,500 over 61 months and zero correlation with patient age ($r = -0.0034$), revenue projections carry high confidence. The lack of single-payer dependence minimizes reimbursement vulnerability.

3. Management Priority Actions
  • Audit Negative Billing: Review the 106 records with negative amounts (-$502 avg) to remediate billing system errors.
  • Reduce 15.5-Day ALOS: Implement discharge coordination protocols to safely improve bed turnaround.
